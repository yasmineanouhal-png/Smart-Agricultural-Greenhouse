# Smart-Agricultural-Greenhouse
An intelligent IoT system for monitoring and automated irrigation of a greenhouse. This system measures in real time the soil moisture, water level, ambient temperature, the electrical consumption of the pump via a current sensor, as well as the color of the plants to assess their health. When a lack of moisture is detected, the water pump and a valve controlled by a servo motor are activated automatically, provided that the water level is sufficient and the temperature does not exceed 35°C, in order to protect the plants. All collected data are transmitted via Bluetooth to a mobile application and simultaneously published on the ThingSpeak platform for remote monitoring and analysis.
